# Hatrack: logo design brief

## What the app is

Hatrack is a small Mac app that lives in the menu bar (the row of icons at the top right of the screen). It lets one person switch between several accounts of the same AI tool with one click, for example a personal account and a company account, without logging out and in again.

Today it switches accounts for the Claude desktop app and the Claude command line tool. Other AI tools will be added later, so the logo should not show or imitate any other company's brand.

## The idea behind the name

A hat rack is where you hang your hats. Each account is a "hat" you wear: a personal hat, a work hat. You pick one off the rack when you need it.

## Who uses it

Developers and other people who use AI tools daily for both work and personal projects. They see the app dozens of times a day, mostly as a tiny icon in the menu bar.

## Personality

- Simple, calm and trustworthy: it handles logins, so it should not look playful to the point of careless.
- A little friendly: the hat idea can bring some warmth.
- Quiet: it sits beside system icons all day and should not shout.

## What we need

Two pieces. They should clearly belong together, but they do not have to be identical.

### 1. App icon (full colour)

Shown in the Applications folder, Launchpad and system dialogs.

- A 1024 × 1024 px PNG with a transparent background, plus the vector source (SVG, Figma or Illustrator).
- Standard macOS shape: a rounded square of about 824 × 824 px centred on the 1024 canvas, leaving a clear margin all round.
- It must still read clearly at 32 × 32 px, so avoid thin lines and small details.
- The smaller sizes are generated from the 1024 file, so only one size is needed.

### 2. Menu bar icon (single colour)

This is the one people see most. It is very small.

- One solid colour only: pure black on a transparent background. No greys, colours, gradients or shadows. macOS recolours it automatically for light and dark menu bars.
- Vector file preferred (SVG or PDF). If PNG: 18 × 18 px and 36 × 36 px.
- The drawing should fit inside about 16 × 16 points, with a 1 point margin on each side.
- Lines at least 1.5 points thick. It must be recognisable at 18 px: please check it at real size.
- It can be a simplified version of the app icon (for example, only the hat).

## Things to avoid

- Any other company's logo, colours or mascot.
- Text or letters that become unreadable when small (a single letter "H" is fine).
- Fine detail, thin strokes, or anything that depends on colour to be understood in the menu bar version.

## Starting points (optional)

Four rough sketches were made to explore directions. They are only references, and the designer is free to go another way:

- A. A hat rack holding two hats of different colours.
- B. Two hats with arrows between them, showing the swap.
- C. A letter "H" wearing a hat.
- D. One simple hat with two dots below it (one filled, one empty) for the active account.
